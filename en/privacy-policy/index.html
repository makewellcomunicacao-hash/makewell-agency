Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 1, 2026

Makewell Comunicação respects your privacy. This policy explains what personal data we process, why, who we share it with, and how you can exercise your rights, in accordance with the Brazilian General Data Protection Law (LGPD, Law No. 13,709/2018) and, for visitors from the European Economic Area, the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR, Regulation (EU) 2016/679).

2. Data protection contact

We are a small-scale processing agent (micro-enterprise) under Brazilian Data Protection Authority Resolution CD/ANPD No. 2/2022 and have therefore not appointed a named Data Protection Officer. Data subjects and the Brazilian National Data Protection Authority (ANPD) can reach us about any data protection matter at contato@makewell.com.br.

3. What data we process

  • Data you send us: name, email, phone number, and the content of your message when you request a diagnosis or get in touch.
  • Browsing data, only with your consent: pages visited, time on site, traffic source, and interactions, collected through analytics and marketing cookies.
  • Technical data: IP address, browser, and device, recorded in the hosting server logs for security purposes.
  • Client data: information needed to provide the contracted services, as set out in the contract (see our Terms of Use).

We do not process sensitive personal data through this website.

6. Who we share data with

We do not sell or rent personal data. We share it only with providers that process data on our behalf or as independent controllers, to the extent necessary:

  • Google (Google Tag Manager, Google Analytics, and Google Ads), if you accept cookies;
  • Microsoft (Microsoft Clarity), if you accept cookies;
  • OpenAI (ads measurement pixel), if you accept cookies;
  • Meta (Meta Pixel, if you accept cookies; and WhatsApp, if you choose to talk to us there);
  • GitHub (GitHub Pages), which hosts the website and records technical logs;
  • YouTube (Google): video thumbnails are loaded from YouTube's servers, which reveals your IP address to them; the video itself only loads when you click, in privacy-enhanced mode (youtube-nocookie.com);
  • public authorities, when required by law or court order.

7. International transfers

Google, Meta, Microsoft, OpenAI, and GitHub may process data in the United States and other countries. These transfers rely on the safeguards provided for in Article 33 of the LGPD and Chapter V of the GDPR, such as standard contractual clauses and, where applicable, these companies' certification under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework.

8. How long we keep data

  • Contacts who did not become clients: up to 2 years after the last contact.
  • Clients: for the duration of the contract and 5 years thereafter, to comply with legal and tax obligations.
  • Cookies: for the periods listed in the table in section 5.
  • Hosting logs: for the period set by the provider.

After these periods, data is deleted or anonymized, unless the law requires us to keep it longer.

9. Your rights

Under the LGPD (Art. 18), you may request: confirmation that we process your data; access; correction of incomplete or outdated data; anonymization, blocking, or deletion of unnecessary data or data processed in breach of the law; portability; deletion of data processed based on consent; information about who we share data with; information about the option of not consenting and its consequences; and withdrawal of consent. You may also file a petition with the ANPD (gov.br/anpd).

Under the GDPR (Arts. 15 to 22), if you are in the European Economic Area: access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, portability, objection, and withdrawal of consent at any time. You may also lodge a complaint with the data protection authority in your country, such as the AEPD (Spain) or the CNPD (Portugal).

United States residents may have rights under applicable state privacy laws, such as the California Consumer Privacy Act as amended by the CPRA (CCPA/CPRA). You can submit requests under these laws through the same email address below.

To exercise any of these rights, write to contato@makewell.com.br. We respond within 15 days (LGPD) or within 1 month (GDPR). We may ask you to confirm your identity to protect your data.

10. Children and adolescents

This website is not directed at anyone under 18, and we do not knowingly collect data from children or adolescents.

11. Security

We adopt technical and organizational measures to protect data against unauthorized access, loss, alteration, or disclosure, such as encrypted connections (HTTPS) and access restricted to team members who need the data.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy to reflect legal changes or changes to our services. The date at the top of this page always indicates the version in effect.

13. Contact

Questions about this policy or your data: contato@makewell.com.br.
