FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Frequently asked questions

How much does it cost to hire Makewell?

Our plans are customized according to the size and goals of each business. The first step is a free diagnosis, where we analyze your business and present a tailored proposal. Contact us via WhatsApp to schedule.

How long does it take to see results?

The first qualified leads usually arrive between the second and third month, once campaigns are calibrated. Between the fourth and sixth month, cost per lead drops and ROI consolidates. Branding and SEO build up cumulatively and gain strength month after month. Our FishWell Method ensures continuous monitoring and clear goals at every stage.

Does Makewell serve businesses outside Brazil?

Yes! We specialize in Brazilian-owned businesses in the United States — especially in Florida, serving clients in Miami, Orlando, Tampa, Jacksonville and throughout the state. We work 100% remotely, so distance is never a barrier. Our team understands both Brazilian business culture and the US market, making us the ideal partner for entrepreneurs who want to grow on both sides.

Which platforms does Makewell manage?

We manage Google Ads, Meta Ads (Instagram and Facebook), SEO, TikTok, LinkedIn and email marketing and CRM strategies. All actions are integrated within the FishWell Method to ensure measurable results.

How does results tracking work?

Each client has access to periodic reports with real metrics: ROI, cost per lead, reach and conversions. We hold regular alignment meetings and you have direct contact with the team responsible for your account — no intermediaries.
